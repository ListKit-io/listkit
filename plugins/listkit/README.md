# ListKit

Run your cold email on ListKit from a conversation with Claude.

## Install

```
/plugin install listkit --marketplace ListKit-io/listkit-agent-kit
```

## Use it

Connect your ListKit account from the plugin's Connectors tab, then tell Claude what you sell and who you sell to. Claude reads your website and account, builds lead lists that cover your whole market, writes your campaign with ListKit's AI Script Writer, launches it on all your inboxes, drafts replies and bookings for your yes, and runs a weekly check that prepares refills so your campaigns never run out of leads. Claude asks for your yes before anything that spends credits, sends email, changes a campaign or can't be undone, including anything a scheduled task prepares.

Try: "Launch my first campaign", "Build me a list of 5,000 dental practice owners", "Is my campaign working?", "Get my inbox drafts ready".

Requires a ListKit Bundle.

## Data

The plugin sends your requests to your ListKit account through ListKit's MCP server (search filters, campaign content, inbox replies and account actions you approve). Feedback about search results goes to ListKit only when you agree, and never includes company names or contact data. ListKit processes that data under its terms and privacy policy at listkit.io. The plugin itself stores nothing.
